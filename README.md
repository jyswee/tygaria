# TYGARIA™

**A multiverse. Three volumes. A world you walk into.**

Tygaria is a multiverse — a connected set of worlds, realms and planes — established
across three volumes of fiction, realised as a computer game, and inhabited as a
persistent virtual world.

It is also the front door. Step through it and you become a **tygazen**: one identity,
yours, that travels across every world and game on the network and is recognisably the
same person in each.

## The three volumes

A trilogy that writes the multiverse into existence: its cosmology, its histories, its
places and the people who live in them. The game and the virtual world are expressions
of what the books establish, not the other way round.

## The world

- **You arrive as yourself.** Your tygazen is built once and comes with you — not a new
  character sheet per title.
- **It is inhabited, not just played.** Other inhabitants are real members of the
  community, and they can talk.
- **Yourself, or someone else entirely.** Your presence here can be close to who you
  actually are, or a deliberate alter ego you keep for this world and nowhere else.
  Both are first-class, and nothing on the page says which you chose.

## PC, web and mobile first. Not VR.

The deliberate departure from how this was attempted before. Lead with hardware and
adoption is gated on buying a headset. Tygaria is entered on the machine you already own,
in the browser you already have — the world comes to you rather than asking you to equip
for it.

And below that line, your **pixel self is your self**. A low-end device does not get a
degraded model or a locked door; it gets the 2D representation that is already yours and
already exactly who you are. What the device can carry decides the renderer. It never
decides who you are.
