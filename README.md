# TYGARIA™

**A multiverse. Three volumes. A world you walk into.**

Tygaria™ is a multiverse: a connected set of worlds, realms and planes, established
across three volumes of fiction, realised as games, and inhabited as a persistent
virtual world.

It is also the front door. Step through it and you become a **tygazen**: one identity,
yours, that travels across every world and game on the network and is recognisably the
same person in each.

## The three volumes

A trilogy that writes the multiverse into existence: its cosmology, its histories, its
places and the people who live in them. The games and the virtual world are expressions
of what the books establish, not the other way round.

1. *The Ironforge Door*
2. *The Nightbloom Door*
3. *The Burned Library*

## The worlds

There was one Tygaria™. Then there were four. All of them meet at **the Crossroads**.

- **Ironforge**, the world that held the siege.
- **Nightbloom**, where the dead are written back.
- **Goldtide**, where everything is for sale.
- **Ashwatch**, the burned library.

## Ways in

- **The pixel world.** Where it began. Playable now at https://tygaria.com/play
- **The city in depth.** The Crossroads and the four worlds as one 3D city with districts
  and shops. In build; not yet open.
- **The arcade district.** The network's first-party games, free to play, at
  https://play.tyga.games/freeplay. The same games stand in the arcades and shops of the
  city. Shop and arcade payments are handled by wesell.cloud as merchant of record.
- **The books.** https://tygaria.com/books

## The world is inhabited

- **You arrive as yourself.** Your tygazen is built once and comes with you, not a new
  character sheet per title.
- **It is inhabited, not just played.** Other inhabitants are members of the community,
  and they can talk.
- **Yourself, or someone else entirely.** Your presence here can be close to who you
  actually are, or a deliberate alter ego you keep for this world and nowhere else.
  Both are first-class, and nothing on the page says which you chose.

## PC, web and mobile first. Not VR.

Tygaria™ is entered on the machine you already own, in the browser you already have.
The world comes to you rather than asking you to equip for it.

Below what a device can carry, your **pixel self is your self**. A low-end device does
not get a degraded model or a locked door; it gets the 2D representation that is already
yours. What the device can carry decides the renderer. It never decides who you are.
